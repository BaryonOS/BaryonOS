# Baryon OS Code of Conduct

## Community commitment

The Baryon OS community is committed to a welcoming, safe, and equitable
environment. Every good-faith participant is entitled to respectful treatment,
regardless of race, ethnicity, caste, colour, age, physical characteristics,
neurodiversity, disability, sex or gender, gender identity or expression,
sexual orientation, language, philosophy or religion, national or social
origin, socio-economic position, education, experience, or other status.

Technical disagreement is expected in an operating-system research project.
Disagreement never justifies harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or
personal attacks.

## Expected behaviour

Participants are expected to:

- communicate honestly, respectfully, and with regard for differences in
  culture, background, language, and experience;
- discuss ideas, evidence, and consequences rather than attacking people;
- give and receive specific, constructive feedback;
- respect stated boundaries and privacy;
- acknowledge mistakes, take responsibility for impact, and help repair harm;
- credit sources and distinguish facts, observations, proposals, and
  assumptions; and
- support the safety and well-being of the community.

## Unacceptable behaviour

The following behaviour is unacceptable, including threats or encouragement of
such behaviour:

- harassment, stalking, intimidation, or continued personal attention after a
  clear request to stop;
- insults, character attacks, demeaning language, or deliberate humiliation;
- discrimination or stereotyping based on identity or personal
  characteristics;
- sexualised language, imagery, or attention inappropriate to the community;
- disclosure or misuse of another person's private information without
  permission;
- threats of violence or other conduct that endangers a person or group;
- impersonation, evasion of moderation measures, or knowingly misleading
  identity claims;
- plagiarism or failure to credit contributed material;
- spam or commercial promotion outside the purpose of the community; and
- retaliation against a person who makes or assists with a good-faith report.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to Baryon OS issues, pull requests, code reviews,
Discussions, repository comments, and other project-managed community spaces.
It also applies when a participant formally represents Baryon OS in another
online or offline space.

The Code of Conduct governs behaviour. Potential security vulnerabilities must
instead be reported through the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md);
exploitable details must not be included in a conduct report, public issue, or
public discussion.

## Reporting a concern

A possible violation can be reported through either of these private routes:

1. Email `github@baryonos.org` with the subject prefix `[Code of Conduct]`.
   Include relevant links, dates, context, and any immediate safety concern.
   Unnecessary personal, medical, or otherwise sensitive information should be
   omitted.
2. For a specific GitHub issue, pull request, Discussion, or comment, prior
   contributors and collaborators can use GitHub's **Report content** action.
   This sends the report to repository administrators. Other participants can
   use the email route above.

The project owner is responsible for project-level moderation. Report details
are shared only as needed to investigate the concern, protect participants,
apply a measure, or meet a legal or GitHub platform requirement. Absolute
confidentiality and a response deadline cannot be guaranteed.

If a report concerns the project owner, or neither project route is safe to
use, report GitHub content directly through
[GitHub's abuse-reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
For conduct on another platform or at an event, use that platform's or event
organiser's independent safety or abuse-reporting route. No independent Baryon
OS moderator is currently appointed, so project-level adjudication may not be
available when the project owner is involved.

Good-faith reports do not violate this Code of Conduct merely because an
investigation does not substantiate the concern.

## Review and enforcement

Reports are assessed on available evidence, context, severity, impact, prior
behaviour, and the safety of affected participants. The person whose conduct is
reported receives only the information needed for a fair response; identifying
details may be withheld when disclosure would create further risk.

Measures may include:

1. **Correction or warning.** A private explanation of the violation and the
   behaviour required to repair it.
2. **Temporary limits.** Time-limited restrictions on specified interactions or
   project spaces.
3. **Temporary suspension.** Removal from community participation for a defined
   period, with conditions for return where appropriate.
4. **Permanent removal.** Loss of access to project-managed community spaces
   when severe harm or repeated violations make lesser measures insufficient.

Steps may be skipped when severity or immediate safety requires it. Moderation
may also include hiding content, locking a conversation, removing material, or
requesting GitHub platform action. Enforcement decisions and evidence are not
public by default; a bounded public explanation may be issued when it can be
done without exposing private information or increasing harm.
