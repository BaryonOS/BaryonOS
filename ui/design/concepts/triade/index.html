A — Triade

Precision instrument · observatory

Idea

A precision instrument for your own matter. The mark's three bonded nodes become live system semantics: every surface can say where your work lives — this device, Thuis-NAS or the internet. Hairline silver geometry on an 8 px grid, Archivo expanded only for identity moments, a deep field by night and observatory daylight by day.

Signature

The Triade instrument in Orbit: three node-lights with icon and label show where work lives and what is moving. The bonded trio returns as who / what / where in the system question (tethered to the instrument), as versions / access / storage in the inspector, and as the focused window's title node. Colour means place only.

Bureaublad — darkDeep-field wallpaper whose three brightest stars are the mark's own triangle; Orbit carries the Triade instrument; continuity waits quietly on the left.
