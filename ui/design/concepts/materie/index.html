Direction C — Materie

Baryons are what matter is made of, so BaryonOS is built like precision hardware. Every window is a machined chassis: a bevelled titanium or anodised-aluminium rim, an engraved title area, and smoked-glass or white-ceramic panes held by metal mullions. Colour never fills a surface: the three colour charges exist only as emissive points of light. A lit slot marks the focused window, a quark light a running app, a shape-coded light where data lives. The wallpaper is the cosmic web, where baryonic matter gathers.

  • Chassis bevelled metal rim, engraved title, panes in mullions; no toolbar
  • Orbit one milled bar; RGB lives only in tiny quark lights
  • Vault opaque, diamond-cut edge, hung from the top edge; origin engraved in the rim
  • Anchoring apps float as rounded chassis; system questions always hang from the top edge, and their origin line states who asks

Bureaublad — dark · Desktop at rest: procedural cosmic-matter wallpaper, milled Orbit, continuity shelf as a small chassis
