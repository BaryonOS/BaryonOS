Orbitaal v2

Direction B · v2 · BaryonOS 2030 · kinetic, atmospheric, spatial

BaryonOS as a quiet piece of physics: layered atmospheric depth, lit by one rim light from the upper left along the mark's −7° lean. Colour now means place only — green for this device, blue for the NAS, red for the internet — and every place or state mark pairs a shape, an icon and text. Pending the project owner's decision, the wallpapers, the marks and the boot keep the three charge colours as identity, not as state. Familiar paths stay intact: Orbit at the bottom centre, a launcher with search, controls on the right.

Signature — light-blades. One tapered, neutral blade marks focus, selection and the active item inside Orbit's top edge. Orbit carries a three-node privacy instrument; trusted questions are opaque, take the opposite theme's material, veil the apps and are tethered to the node that asks.

Bureaublad — dark. v2: recent work on a calm frosted plate with its trajectory in the gutter; a three-node privacy instrument; one neutral blade marks the active item.
