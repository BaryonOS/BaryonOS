Orbitaal

Direction B · BaryonOS 2030 · kinetic, atmospheric, spatial

BaryonOS as a quiet piece of physics. Every surface lives in layered atmospheric depth — nebula indigo at night, upper atmosphere at dawn by day — lit by one rim light from the upper left, along the mark's −7° lean. Colour is rationed: the three charges glow faintly in the wallpaper and bind toward a neutral core, like the mark converging; in the interface they only mark energy, state and the active app. Familiar paths stay intact: dock at the bottom centre, launcher with search, controls on the right.

Signature — orbital light. The mark's tapered blades become light-blades: one tapered luminous stroke draws focus, selection and the active app, and nothing else. Orbit, the dock, floats under a thin orbital arc whose blade tracks the running app. Trusted questions are opaque, rise from Orbit and are tethered to its privacy indicator.

Bureaublad — dark. Wallpaper "Binding": three charges glow and bind toward a neutral core. Recent work sits on a trajectory; Orbit's arc rests over the mark.
