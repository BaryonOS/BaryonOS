# Source register and verification limits

Access date for all sources listed below: **2 November 2026**, Europe/Amsterdam. The original publication/modification date is stated separately; unknown does not mean recently published. This register is a navigable index, not a statement that every page of every report was assessed. The specific passages used and their limitations are recorded with the research findings.

Sources from the same vendor or the same study do not count as independent confirmations. A Commission press release, the associated report and the table provide three ways to check **one survey**, not three studies. A GitHub issue is an original report, not a representative sample. A vendor roadmap establishes an intention or acknowledged area of concern, not broad availability or effects on users.

## Reproducibility

The original Eurobarometer PDFs were retrieved through the download handler from the official publication records, read as text with `pypdf` from the bundled runtime, and rendered with Poppler at the relevant table/report page. The QB6.4 columns and EU27 row were checked visually. Poppler produced font-substitution warnings; the checked labels/figures were readable and matched the text extraction. Exact hashes, sizes and locations are recorded in [eurobarometer-check.json](eurobarometer-check.json).

The general web text reader could not open some DOI/PDF download routes. This was recorded as an access limitation; the original files proved directly accessible through the official download route. Inaccessible full text of other studies is marked per report as a check limited to the abstract/metadata/institutional summary. A search-result snippet does not replace content that has been read.

No complete source texts, personal data, accounts, cookies, secrets or temporary command logs are included in the research directory. Links can change later; date, source identity and file hashes for the main claim make drift visible. The literature research is targeted, not exhaustive or preregistered.
