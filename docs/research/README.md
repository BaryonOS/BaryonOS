## Preserved 28 October studies

This directory preserves the repository adaptation of two studies from 28 October 2026:

- market research into gaps in the desktop OS market, European alternatives, design/performance trends, positioning and sustained switching;
- consumer research into adoption, habit, switching friction, information management, privacy, visual experience, notifications and local AI.

The full reports remain preserved as separate research artifacts. This repository document summarizes the findings for use in product and development work without silently turning research proposals into product requirements.

<a id="relatie-tot-bestaande-projectbesluiten"></a>

## Relationship to existing project decisions

The studies **confirm or support** several existing choices:

- Materiaal as the object/document-centered product direction;
- privacy and ease of use as a joint acceptance condition;
- local AI without cloud fallback;
- local or end-to-end encrypted personal storage;
- explicit, bounded app permissions;
- European control as a product orientation;
- a polished, high-quality interface rather than a privacy product that lags technically or visually.

These points were therefore not new on 28 October 2026.

<a id="wat-de-onderzoeken-wél-nieuw-toevoegen"></a>

## What the studies add

The main additions to the repository are external support and market interpretation:

1. current desktop market shares and European attitudes toward digital dependence;
2. evidence that dissatisfaction with an existing OS does not automatically lead to switching;
3. a comparison of European alternatives and lessons from their limited scale;
4. switching, compatibility, hardware validation, recovery and support as product components;
5. scientific support for habit, switching costs, first impressions, personal information management, privacy choices and continued use;
6. measurable hypotheses for performance and continued use;
7. a research route from task interviews through usability tests to a 90-/180-day pilot.

<a id="niet-automatisch-bindend"></a>

## Not automatically binding

The following proposals remain hypotheses until confirmed by user research, technical measurement or an explicit project decision:

- the precise initial target audience;
- color palettes and visual variants;
- latency, wake and search targets;
- sample sizes;
- a retention threshold such as 75%;
- sales through specific hardware or service partners;
- pricing and revenue models.

If such a hypothesis is adopted as a product requirement, record that change separately in `DECISIONS.md`, `DESIGN-AMBITION.md` or the relevant implementation specification.
