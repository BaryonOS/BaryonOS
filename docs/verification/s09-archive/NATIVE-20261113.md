**Earlier runs (not evidence of a pass).**
1. **Run 1 (export failed on the host):** the runner compared the archive
   generation with the global volume generation. The header carries LIST's
   domain generation: 58 = the sum of the domain-0 revisions. The check was
   corrected.
2. **Run 2 (corrupt failed at guest step 740):** export and restore passed, but
   the portal's first archive read met the service's mount I/O and received
   Busy instead of `Corrupt`. The fix uses ordering, not retries: the portal
   first reaches a READY service, and slot 2 waits for kernel gate 2.
