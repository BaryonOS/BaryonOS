What is the central research question?

Baryon OS investigates whether an independently developed kernel, services and product experience can form a useful operating system while preserving clear authority over data. Design proposals, host experiments, native QEMU results and physical hardware tests answer different questions; none can stand in for a supported daily-use release.

What is the local AI goal?

System AI is required to run on the user's device, with bounded access to selected data and no inference network permission or silent cloud fallback. Host-side model and numerical foundations exist, but a useful native inference task and its network-denial acceptance remain open.

What does quantum research include?

The work separates post-quantum transport, local quantum computation and possible hybrid hardware. A native QEMU software simulator and job interface have bounded acceptance evidence for reference circuits, quotas and cancellation. This does not demonstrate a physical quantum processing unit, quantum speedup or general hardware support. Post-quantum transport and an OS control layer for hybrid hardware remain separate research and integration work.
