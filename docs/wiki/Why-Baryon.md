# Why Baryon?

The name refers to baryons, including protons and neutrons. The [name explanation in the README](https://github.com/simonvanieperen/BaryonOS/blob/main/README.md#waarom-de-naam-baryon-os) gives the scientific references and the project metaphor: a small kernel, separate services and a user environment form a coherent whole.

The metaphor is about composition. It does not specify a number of CPU privilege rings, establish physical properties of software, or demonstrate safety or stability. Those questions require the [security contract](https://github.com/simonvanieperen/BaryonOS/blob/main/docs/implementation/KERNEL-AND-SECURITY-CONTRACT.md) and scoped [test evidence](https://github.com/simonvanieperen/BaryonOS/tree/main/docs/verification).
