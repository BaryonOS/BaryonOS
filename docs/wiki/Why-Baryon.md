# Why Baryon?

The name refers to baryons, a particle category in which the Particle Data Group lists the proton (`p`) and neutron (`n`). The [name explanation in the README](https://github.com/BaryonOS/BaryonOS/blob/main/README.md#waarom-de-naam-baryon-os) gives the project metaphor: a small kernel, separate services and a user environment form a coherent whole.

The metaphor is about composition. It does not specify a number of CPU privilege rings, establish physical properties of software, or demonstrate safety or stability. Those questions require the [security contract](https://github.com/BaryonOS/BaryonOS/blob/main/docs/implementation/KERNEL-AND-SECURITY-CONTRACT.md) and scoped [test evidence](https://github.com/BaryonOS/BaryonOS/tree/main/docs/verification).

## Scientific source

- F. Takahashi et al. (Particle Data Group). *Review of Particle Physics*, 2026 edition, *International Journal of Modern Physics A* 41, 2630011 (2026), [Particle Properties](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2026/listings/particle_properties.html).
