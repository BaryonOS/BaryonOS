# Research Tracks

Research explores possibilities; it is not a support commitment. The [research index](https://github.com/simonvanieperen/BaryonOS/blob/main/docs/research/README.md) provides the source material and dates.

- **Quantum:** distinguish post-quantum protection, local quantum simulation/execution research and the OS as a control layer for hybrid hardware. A simulator is not a physical QPU or proof of speedup. See [the quantum research scope](https://github.com/simonvanieperen/BaryonOS/blob/main/docs/QUANTUM-RESEARCH.md) and [the quantum specification](https://github.com/simonvanieperen/BaryonOS/blob/main/docs/implementation/QUANTUM-SPEC.md).
- **Local AI:** the product direction calls for on-device assistance within data and permission boundaries. See [the product specification](https://github.com/simonvanieperen/BaryonOS/blob/main/docs/implementation/PRODUCT-SPEC.md) and [privacy requirements](https://github.com/simonvanieperen/BaryonOS/blob/main/docs/LOCAL-PRIVACY-CONTRACT.md); a model experiment does not establish native integration.
- **Future architectures:** second-ISA exploration belongs to the [roadmap](https://github.com/simonvanieperen/BaryonOS/blob/main/docs/ROADMAP.md); hardware-assisted isolation research belongs to [the verifiable security specification](https://github.com/simonvanieperen/BaryonOS/blob/main/docs/implementation/VERIFIABLE-SECURITY-AND-PQC.md). Neither establishes general hardware support.

Host prototype, own-OS port, simulator and physical hardware evidence must be labeled separately. Current integration belongs in [PROJECT-STATE](https://github.com/simonvanieperen/BaryonOS/blob/main/docs/PROJECT-STATE.md).
